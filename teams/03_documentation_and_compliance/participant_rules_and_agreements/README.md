## 1. Scope & Mandate

This folder houses the legal agreements, behavioral policies, intellectual property declarations, and safety undertakings that govern every student hacker attending KAIROS 2027.

Under the constitution of the AWS Student Builder Group — SIST Chapter:
1. **100% Student IP Ownership:** The student inventors retain 100% unrestricted intellectual property ownership of all source code, models, datasets, and architectures created during the hackathon. Neither Sathyabama nor any corporate sponsor has an automatic claim on student IP.
2. **Zero Tolerance Code of Conduct:** Absolute prohibition against harassment, discrimination, plagiarism, or malicious cyber exploitation.
3. **Overnight Safety Undertaking:** Mandatory consent and acknowledgement of the 10:00 PM – 06:00 AM secure night lock-in protocol.

## 3. Policy Enforcement & Disqualification Thresholds
- **Pre-Existing Code:** All functional software code must be authored during the official 48-hour hacking window. Libraries, open-source SDKs, and foundational models are permitted, provided they are declared during Milestone Gate 1.
- **Git Commit Audits:** Repositories must maintain regular public commits. Bulk commits of completed applications will trigger automatic disqualification by the Technical Jury.
- **Lock-In Violations:** Any student leaving the Central Library perimeter between 10:00 PM and 06:00 AM without medical or executive escort is immediately disqualified and reported to university authorities.
