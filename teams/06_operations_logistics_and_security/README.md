## 1. Team Charter & Operational Scope

### 1.1 Mission & Venue Scope
Division 6 is the physical operational backbone of KAIROS 2027. Commanding the massive **Sathyabama Indoor Stadium (Indoor Auditorium)** (~2,500 seating capacity), this vertical orchestrates venue infrastructure, power distribution, campus transportation, 8-service full-board catering, and 24/7 security/medical protection for 500+ attendees over 48 continuous hours.

### 1.2 Core Mandates & Operational KPIs
- **Uninterrupted 65 kVA Power Grid:** 100 round table pods supplied with spike-protected power drops backed by dual 125 kVA auto-synchronized diesel generators (< 10ms failover).
- **High-Density Wi-Fi 6 Mesh:** 18–20 Ruckus/Aruba Wi-Fi 6 APs sustaining 1,000+ concurrent active devices with zero packet loss.
- **100% Free Full-Board Catering:** 8 complimentary food services (6 major buffets, 2 evening high teas, 01:30 AM midnight pizza/energy drops) plus 24/7 mineral water canisters and tea/coffee stations.
- **Fleet Mobility:** 10 Sathyabama College Bus routes spanning Chennai transit hubs and 3 Green Campus EV shuttles running continuous campus loops.
- **Zero Safety Incidents:** 100% adherence to the **10:00 PM – 06:00 AM Night Lock-In**, Green Room 1 female rest sanctuary, and 24/7 medical standby liaison with Sathyabama General Hospital.

### 1.3 Operational Boundaries (Scope of Control)
- **What This Team Controls:** Physical arena bays Alpha–Echo, electrical cable ramps, table linen dressing, college mess catering requisition, midnight snack distribution, bus routes, EV buggies, night gate security, medical triage.
- **What This Team Does NOT Control:**
  - *No Graphic Printing:* Standee, banner, and badge graphic design is owned by Division 2 (Design). Operations mounts and positions finished prints.
  - *No Software/Judge Scoring:* Judging portal operation and leaderboard scoring are owned by Division 5 (Builder Squad).
  - *No Commercial Negotiations:* Food and material procurement budgets are audited and approved by Nikki (Sponsorship Lead) (Division 1).

## 4. Operations Lifecycle & Shift System

### 4.1 5-Phase Operations Roadmap
1. **Phase 1: Institutional Approvals (T-90 to T-30 Days):** Requisition Letters 01–06 processed with Estate, Transport, and Mess offices.
2. **Phase 2: Resource Allocation (T-30 to T-7 Days):** Bus route driver rosters, 100 extension boards, 500 chairs, and water canisters secured.
3. **Phase 3: Setup Day & Load Testing (T-24 Hours / Jan 21):** Arena power grid assembled; 65 kVA dummy load test conducted; generator auto-switch tested (< 10ms).
4. **Phase 4: Live 48H Hackathon Execution (Jan 22–24):** 3 rotating shifts active; 8 food buffets served on the minute; night lock-in enforced.
5. **Phase 5: Teardown & Venue Handback (T+12 Hours / Jan 24 Evening):** Arena tables folded, extension boards audited, stadium floor cleaned and returned to Estate Office.

### 4.2 3-Shift Volunteer Rotation (8-Hour Cycles)
- **Shift 1 (Day Prime):** 08:00 AM – 04:00 PM (35 volunteers) · Influx, lunch buffet, mentor rounds.
- **Shift 2 (Evening Sprint):** 04:00 PM – 12:00 AM (35 volunteers) · High tea, dinner buffet, checkpoint 1.
- **Shift 3 (Graveyard Fuel & Security):** 12:00 AM – 08:00 AM (25 volunteers) · Midnight snacks, perimeter lock-in, quiet pod monitoring.
- **NOC Handover Protocol:** Mandatory 15-minute overlap briefing at Stadium Foyer NOC between incoming and outgoing shift marshals.
