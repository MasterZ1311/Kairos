## 1. Scope & Mandate

This folder directs the security deployments, incident command hierarchies, perimeter access controls, medical emergency responses, and the strict **10:00 PM – 06:00 AM Night Lock-In** enforcing complete campus discipline and safety for 500+ participants.

Following institutional approval, all security, resting, and medical protocols are centered at the **Sathyabama Indoor Stadium (Indoor Auditorium)**.

## 2. Night Lock-In Protocol & Physical Security Perimeter

To ensure 100% safety for all national participants, especially female students and outstation attendees:
1. **Curfew Hours:** Strictly active between **10:00 PM and 06:00 AM** on the nights of **January 22 and January 23, 2027**.
2. **Lock-In Perimeter:** All participants must remain inside the secure Sathyabama Indoor Stadium complex. External arena access doors are manned 24/7 by institutional security guards and senior student organizers.
3. **Segregated Rest Sanctuaries & Facilities:**
   - **Backstage Green Room 1 (Female Rest Sanctuary):** Dedicated, secure sanctuary with air conditioning, attached private restrooms, cushioned rest couches, and 24/7 supervision by female faculty wardens (Dr. Balapriya S & lady wardens) and female security guards.
   - **Upper Bleachers Mezzanine (Male Quiet Nap Pods):** 40 padded sleeping mats designated as a 100% silent rest zone for rotating attendee nap shifts.
4. **Zero Roaming Rule:** Wandering outside the stadium complex past 10:00 PM is strictly prohibited without an authorized organizer escort. Violators face immediate disqualification and disciplinary referral.
