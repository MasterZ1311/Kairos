## 1. Scope & Mandate

Under the **100% Free / Zero Entry Fee** model of KAIROS 2027, this folder commands the nutrition, hydration, and hospitality operations that sustain 500+ participants (100 teams / 450–600 hackers), 25+ industry judges and evaluators, and 106 student organizers for 48 non-stop hours.

Hospitality Scope:
- **8 Complimentary Food Services:**
  - **6 Full-Board Buffet Meals** served across campus central mess halls (Day 1 Lunch & Dinner; Day 2 Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner; Day 3 Breakfast & Grand Celebration Lunch).
  - **2 Evening High Teas & Hot Snacks** (fresh samosas, vegetable cutlets, South Indian filter coffee, and masala tea).
  - **2 Midnight Fuel Drops (01:30 AM):** Fresh personal pan pizzas, cheesy subs, energy bars, and dark roast coffee drops delivered directly to arena hack bays.
- **Continuous 24/7 Hydration & Coffee Station:** Bean-to-cup filter coffee dispensers and 20L mineral water stations situated across Sathyabama Indoor Stadium arena floor and foyer.
- **Backstage Green Room 2 VIP Executive Dining:** Specialized multi-course executive catering for corporate judges, CXOs, venture partners, and university dignitaries.

## 2. Campus Dining Logistics (Boy & Girl Mess Halls)

To align with campus safety protocols and maintain discipline:
- **Meal Transit:** Attendees walk or take continuous 8-seater Campus EV Buggies (`EV-01`) from Sathyabama Indoor Stadium to the dining complexes.
- **Segregated Dining Halls:**
  - **Male Participants:** Men's Central Mess Dining Hall.
  - **Female Participants:** Women's Central Mess Dining Hall, escorted by female student organizers and faculty wardens.
- **Dining Pass:** Verified via Smart NFC Badge tap at the mess turnstile gate.
