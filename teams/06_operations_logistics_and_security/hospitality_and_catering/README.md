## 1. Scope & Mandate

Under the **100% Free / Zero Entry Fee** model of KAIROS 2027, this folder commands the nutrition, hydration, and hospitality operations that sustain 500+ participants, 25+ industry judges and evaluators, and 100 student organizers for 48 non-stop hours.

Hospitality Scope:
- **6 Full-Board Buffet Meals** served across campus mess halls (Day 1 Lunch & Dinner; Day 2 Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner; Day 3 Breakfast & Lunch).
- **Evening High Tea & Hot Snacks** (fresh samosas, vegetable cutlets, tea/coffee).
- **01:30 AM Midnight Fuel Drops** (fresh Domino's/Pizza Hut hot pizza boxes, cookies, bananas, high-caffeine beverages).
- **Continuous 24/7 Hydration & Filter Coffee:** Self-service automatic bean-to-cup coffee machines and 20L mineral water dispensers stationed on all 3 floors of the Central Library.
- **Top Floor VIP Executive Dining:** Specialized continental and traditional South Indian banquet catering for corporate judges, CXOs, and university dignitaries.

---

## 2. Segregated Dining Logistics (Boy & Girl Mess Halls)

To align with campus safety protocols and maintain discipline:
- **Male Participants:** Routed via the **North Stairwell** of the Central Library directly to the **Men's Central Mess Dining Hall**.
- **Female Participants:** Routed via the **South Stairwell** accompanied by female faculty coordinators and student wardens to the **Women's Mess Dining Hall**.
- **Dining Pass:** Verified via Smart NFC Badge tap at the mess gate.
