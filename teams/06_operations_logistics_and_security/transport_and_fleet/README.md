## 1. Scope & Mandate

This folder manages the transportation logistics for outstation and local participants attending KAIROS 2027 under our **100% Free Participant Model**.

Transportation infrastructure deployed:
1. **10 Sathyabama College Bus Routes:** Deployed across major Chennai transport junctions (Central/Egmore, Tambaram, CMBT, Velachery, Chengalpattu, Avadi, Poonamallee, Red Hills, ECR, Navalur) on the morning of January 22, 2027 (arrival by 09:15 AM), and post-valedictory dispersal on January 24, 2027 (departure at 05:30 PM).
2. **3 Campus Electric Vehicle (EV) Buggies:** Operating continuously inside the Sathyabama campus for passenger shuttle service between the Sathyabama Indoor Stadium, student mess halls, guest house, and the main campus gate.

## 3. Campus EV Buggy Safety & Night Operation Rules
- The 3 EV buggies operate **24 hours continuously** from 08:00 AM on Jan 22 until 05:00 PM on Jan 24.
- **Fleet Allocation:**
  - **EV-01 (8-Seater):** Continuous attendee shuttle between Sathyabama Indoor Stadium, Student Mess Halls, and Main Campus Gate.
  - **EV-02 (6-Seater Executive):** Dedicated VIP jury, keynote speaker, and corporate sponsor shuttle between University Guest House and Indoor Stadium.
  - **EV-03 (6-Seater Utility/Emergency):** Rapid response vehicle dedicated to medical transfer liaison with Sathyabama General Hospital, late-night female organizer/participant escorts, and heavy catering logistics.
- During the **10:00 PM – 06:00 AM Night Lock-In**, EV buggies operate strictly under organizer escort and campus security supervision.
