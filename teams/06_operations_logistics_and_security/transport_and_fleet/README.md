## 1. Scope & Mandate

This folder manages the transportation logistics for outstation and local participants attending KAIROS 2027 under our **100% Free Participant Model**.

Transportation infrastructure deployed:
1. **10 Sathyabama College Bus Routes:** Deployed across major Chennai transport junctions on the morning of January 22, 2027, and post-valedictory dispersal on January 24, 2027.
2. **2 Campus Electric Vehicle (EV) Buggies:** Operating continuously inside the Sathyabama campus for passenger shuttle service between the Central Library, student mess halls, hostel accommodation blocks, and the main campus gate.

## 3. Campus EV Buggy Safety & Night Operation Rules
- The 2 EV buggies operate **24 hours continuously** from 08:00 AM on Jan 22 until 05:00 PM on Jan 24.
- During the **10:00 PM – 06:00 AM Night Lock-In**, EV buggies are reserved strictly for:
  - Escorting female participants from the library to girls' hostel rest rooms under security supervision.
  - Emergency transit to the on-campus Apollo Medical Clinic.
  - Moving heavy catering supplies (midnight pizza boxes, water canisters).
