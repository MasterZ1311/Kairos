## 3. On-Site Proof of Performance (PoP) Verification Protocol

To ensure sponsors renew for future editions and release formal post-event testimonials:

1. **Dedicated DSLR Photographer Assignment:** Assign one designated photographer from Division 5 (Media & Production) to capture high-resolution photographic evidence of:
   - Corporate executive delivering keynote address on the main stage.
   - Corporate crest rendered on the 3840×1920 LED Wall behind speakers.
   - Participants interacting at the corporate sponsor exhibition kiosk in the grand foyer.
   - Macro close-ups of student hackers wearing lanyards and smart badges with sponsor logos.
   - Corporate judges actively evaluating and scoring finalist squads.
2. **Post-Event Executive Dossier:** Compile all photographic evidence, YouTube live stream view telemetry, and opt-in candidate engagement metrics into a 10-slide executive presentation delivered within **7 business days** post-event.
