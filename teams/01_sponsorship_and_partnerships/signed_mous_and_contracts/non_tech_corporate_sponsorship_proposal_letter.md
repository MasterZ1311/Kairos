### About KAIROS 2027 (Grand Edition)
KAIROS 2027 is a rigorous **48-hour continuous non-stop national hackathon** bringing together the top 5% of student engineering talent in India. Enforcing a strict **100% Free / Zero Entry Fee** mandate to guarantee uncompromised meritocracy, the summit targets an applicant pool of **over 2,000+ candidate teams** from premier institutions nationwide (including IITs, NITs, BITS, and leading state universities across 20+ Indian states). 

Following a rigorous dual-phase evaluation—commencing with an intensive virtual screening round featuring working code commits and live technical interviews—only the **Top 100 Elite National Teams (~400–600 developers)** are invited to the physical grand finale on our campus for 48 hours of uninterrupted engineering.
