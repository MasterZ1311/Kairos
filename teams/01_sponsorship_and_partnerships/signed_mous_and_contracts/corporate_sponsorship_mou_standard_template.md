The Organizer agrees to deliver the following agreed benefits in full:
1. **Brand Exposure:** Prominent logo placement on the Main Stage LED Wall (3840×1920 px), stage backdrop banner, foyer rollup standees, and participant ID badges as per the agreed package tier.
2. **Keynote & Speaking Slots:** Dedicated speaking slot during the Inaugural or Plenary sessions as defined in the package schedule.
3. **Jury & Mentorship Allocation:** [___] designated evaluation seat(s) on the Grand Finale or Track Evaluation Panel.
4. **Talent & Recruitment Engagement:** Provision of the verified **Opt-In Finalist Candidate Book** (containing GitHub profiles and resumes of participants who have explicitly consented to share their profiles with corporate partners in compliance with data privacy standards).
5. **Live Stream Integration:** Video commercial stingers or lower-third graphic overlays broadcasted during the 48-hour continuous multicam live stream.
6. **Physical Exhibition Kiosk:** Allocation of exhibition stall space in the Sathyabama Indoor Stadium foyer during the hackathon.

### 4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE SPONSOR
1. Provide high-resolution vector logos (SVG/AI/EPS) within 5 business days of MoU signing (and no later than the December 20, 2026 print freeze).
2. Provide technical problem statement details, APIs, or sandbox credentials (if sponsoring a dedicated track challenge).
3. Nominate designated jury members or evaluators by January 10, 2027.
4. Transfer agreed financial consideration according to Section 2.

### 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY & CODE RIGHTS
It is mutually and expressly affirmed that **all intellectual property (IP), source code, patents, and technical designs created by student participant teams during KAIROS 2027 remain 100% the exclusive property of the student participants**. The Sponsor acquires zero automatic ownership of participant code. The Sponsor may negotiate independent licenses, internships, or commercial terms directly with participant teams post-event without institutional interference.

---

### 6. DATA PRIVACY & COMPLIANCE
Both parties agree to handle all personal student data in strict compliance with applicable data privacy legislation and institutional policy. The Organizer warrants that candidate directories provided to the Sponsor contain only details of participants who have explicitly granted opt-in consent for corporate recruitment.
