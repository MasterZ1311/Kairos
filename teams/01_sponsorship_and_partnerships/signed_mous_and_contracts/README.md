## 1. Scope & Mandate

This directory serves as the legal and contractual vault for KAIROS 2027. It stores executed Memorandums of Understanding (MoUs), corporate partnership agreements, GST tax invoices raised, and bank payment confirmation receipts.

### Legal Governance Standards
- All agreements must be executed on official university letterheads or formal corporate tripartite agreement templates.
- Sponsorship funds are routed directly into the designated **Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology Official Project Bank Account** (strictly audited).
- Pure cash prize pool funds are held in verifiable escrow and disbursed via automated NEFT within 72 hours of hackathon grand finale conclusion.

## 3. Standard Contract File Naming Convention

Every finalized agreement must be stored in PDF format following this strict taxonomy:
- `MOU_[Company_Name]_[Tier]_[Amount]_SIGNED.pdf` (e.g., `MOU_Presidio_CoPoweredBy_250K_SIGNED.pdf`)
- `INV_[Invoice_No]_[Company_Name]_[Date].pdf` (e.g., `INV_SIST_2026_094_AWS_150K.pdf`)
- `UTR_[Company_Name]_[Transaction_ID].pdf` (e.g., `UTR_Presidio_HDFC294819482.pdf`)
