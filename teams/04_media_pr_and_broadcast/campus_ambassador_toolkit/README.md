## 2. Ambassador & Student Incentive Structure

### 2.1 Policy on Corporate Sponsorship Referral Commissions
> [!WARNING]
> **GOVERNANCE DIRECTIVE: REFERRAL CASH COMMISSIONS QUARANTINED**  
> **Status:** `[DECISION REQUIRED / APPROVAL REQUIRED — DO NOT OFFER]` (DEC-05)  
> 
> A prior concept proposed paying 10%–15% cash commissions ("Rainmaker Bounties") to students sourcing sponsors. This policy is **NOT active**. Offering direct cash commissions without formal University Finance, Registrar, and Vice-Chancellor approval violates university accounts rules and statutory tax laws (TDS/GST).  
> 
> **Operating Instruction:** Campus Ambassadors and student organizers must **NOT** promise or advertise cash referral commissions. Corporate partnerships operate strictly through official university MoUs. Ambassador achievements are rewarded through prestigious academic recognition, builder merchandise, and stage honors as detailed below.

### 2.2 Student Team Registration Milestones & Recognition
To drive relentless student engagement and outreach across peer colleges, ambassadors achieve tiered milestone rewards based on verified team applications:

| Tier Level | Qualified Team Referrals | Ambassador Rewards & Privileges |
|:---:|:---:|---|
| **Tier 1: Bronze Builder** | 3 – 5 Teams | Official Certificate of Merit from Sathyabama & AWS SBGL + Verifiable Digital Badge |
| **Tier 2: Silver Architect** | 6 – 10 Teams | Exclusive AWS SBG Official Streetwear Tee + Fast-Track Semi-Final Review |
| **Tier 3: Gold Evangelist** | 11 – 20 Teams | AWS SBG Heavyweight Crewneck Hoodie + Direct VIP All-Access Pass to Grand Finale |
| **Tier 4: Apex Ambassador** | 20+ Teams | Grand Finale Travel Reimbursement + 1-on-1 Cloud Architecture Review with AWS Solutions Architects + Stage Recognition Award |
