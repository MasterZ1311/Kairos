# PRESS RELEASE: FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**HEADLINE:**  
### SATHYABAMA'S AWS STUDENT BUILDER GROUP ANNOUNCES "KAIROS 2027" — A NATIONWIDE 48-HOUR ZERO-FEE HACKATHON WITH ₹10 LAKH+ UNCAPPED CAPITAL POOL

**DATELINE:** CHENNAI, TAMIL NADU — OCTOBER 15, 2026

**CHENNAI, INDIA** — Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology, in formal collaboration with the Worldwide Amazon Web Services (AWS) Student Builder Groups Program, has officially launched **KAIROS 2027 (Grand Edition)**, set to take place from **January 22 to 24, 2027** at the iconic Central Library Complex in Chennai.

Structured as a grueling **48-hour non-stop continuous engineering sprint**, KAIROS 2027 represents a seismic shift in student-led technical summits. Enforcing a radical **"100% Free / Zero Entry Fee"** mandate, the hackathon guarantees that every shortlisted student builder receives complimentary full-board hospitality, university fleet transit, developer kits, and cloud infrastructure access, supported by a **minimum ₹10,00,000 uncapped corporate capital pool** and an escrowed pure cash prize pool exceeding **₹3,50,000+**.

The summit is anchored by **Thenappan T**, President and Student Builder Group Leader (SBGL), under the institutional mentorship of Faculty Coordinators **Dr. Balapriya .S** and **Dr. K. Ashok Kumar**, with executive patronship from Dean **Dr. L. Lakshmanan** and Vice Chancellor **Dr. T. Sasipraba**.

*"KAIROS 2027 is engineered to eliminate every financial barrier standing between extraordinary student intellect and world-class cloud deployment,"* said Thenappan T. *"We are deploying a rigorous two-tier selection funnel—filtering over 2,000 national applicants down to an online semi-final of 250 teams, and ultimately convening the top 100 elite engineering squads on our campus to solve high-consequence challenges using Amazon Bedrock foundation models, serverless architectures, and next-generation decentralized networks."*

The hackathon features five core innovation tracks:
1. **Agentic AI & Foundation Models** (AWS Bedrock, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Autonomous Multi-Agent Swarms)
2. **Cloud-Native Resilient Systems** (Serverless Architectures, High-Concurrency Microservices)
3. **Decentralized Infrastructure & Cryptographic Privacy** (Web3 & Zero-Knowledge Systems)
4. **Next-Gen FinTech & High-Frequency APIs** (Algorithmic Financial Infrastructure)
5. **Smart Healthcare & Ambient Intelligence** (Edge Telemetry & Healthcare ML)

The Grand Finale will be hosted inside the Central Library Complex, featuring dedicated high-density computing bays, dual-generator power redundancy, and continuous 48-hour multicam live streaming. The Grand Champion team will take home **₹1,50,000 in pure cash**, grand trophies, and venture capital acceleration pathways.

National applications are now officially open at **https://kairos.sistawscc.org** and close on **December 15, 2026**.
