# PRESS RELEASE: FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**HEADLINE:**  
### SATHYABAMA'S AWS STUDENT BUILDER GROUP ANNOUNCES "KAIROS 2027" — A NATIONWIDE 48-HOUR ZERO-FEE HACKATHON WITH ₹1,75,000 PURE CASH PRIZE ESCROW

**DATELINE:** CHENNAI, TAMIL NADU — OCTOBER 15, 2026

**CHENNAI, INDIA** — Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology, in formal collaboration with the Worldwide Amazon Web Services (AWS) Student Builder Groups Program, has officially launched **KAIROS 2027 (Grand Edition)**, set to take place from **January 22 to 24, 2027** at the **Sathyabama Indoor Stadium (Indoor Auditorium)**, Sathyabama Campus, Chennai.

Structured as a grueling **48-hour non-stop continuous engineering sprint**, KAIROS 2027 represents a seismic shift in student-led technical summits. Enforcing a radical **"100% Free / Zero Entry Fee"** mandate, the hackathon guarantees that every shortlisted student builder receives complimentary full-board hospitality, university fleet transit, developer kits, and cloud infrastructure access, supported by a **₹3,50,000 – ₹4,00,000 corporate capital pool** and an escrowed pure cash prize pool of **₹1,75,000**.

The summit is anchored by **Thenappan T**, President and Student Builder Group Leader (SBGL), under the institutional guidance of Faculty Coordinators **Dr. K. Ashok Kumar** and **Dr. Balapriya .S**, with executive patronship from Dean **Dr. L. Lakshmanan** and Vice Chancellor **Dr. T. Sasipraba**.

*"KAIROS 2027 is engineered to eliminate every financial barrier standing between extraordinary student intellect and world-class cloud deployment,"* said Thenappan T. *"We are deploying a rigorous two-tier selection funnel—filtering over 2,000 national applicants down to an online semi-final of 250 teams, and ultimately convening the top 100 elite engineering squads on our campus to solve high-consequence challenges using Amazon Bedrock foundation models, serverless architectures, and next-generation decentralized networks."*

The hackathon features five core innovation tracks:
1. **Agentic AI & Next-Gen Autonomous Systems** (AWS Bedrock, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Autonomous Multi-Agent Swarms)
2. **Cloud-Native, Serverless & Distributed Systems** (AWS Lambda, Fargate, High-Throughput Microservices)
3. **Web3, Decentralized Identity & Zero-Trust Security** (Self-Sovereign Identity, Smart Contracts & Zero-Knowledge)
4. **FinTech, High-Frequency Systems & Algorithmic Trust** (Algorithmic Trading, Fraud Prevention & Automated Settlement)
5. **Open Enterprise & Sustainable Engineering Solutions** (CleanTech, Intelligent Mobility & Healthcare Telemetry)

The Grand Finale will be hosted at the state-of-the-art **Sathyabama Indoor Stadium**, featuring dedicated high-density computing bays (Bays A–D), 65 kVA electrical distribution, dual-generator power redundancy (<10ms switchover), and continuous 48-hour multicam live streaming. The Grand Champion team will take home **₹60,000 in pure cash**, custom acrylic hourglass trophies, and venture capital acceleration pathways.

National applications are now officially open at **https://kairos.sathyabama.ac.in** and close on **December 15, 2026**.
