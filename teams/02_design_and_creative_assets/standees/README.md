```text
Substrate: 280 Micron Non-Curl Polypropylene Synthetic Film (Matte Finish)
Base Hardware: Premium Silver Anodized Aluminum with Twin Swivel Feet & Padded Carry Bag
Top Bar: Clamp-type Aluminum Profile (no adhesive peeling)
Support Pole: 3-Stage Telescopic Shock-Corded Pole
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

### Safety & Layout Guidelines:
- **Top Safe Margin:** Keep 2 inches (600 px at 300 DPI) clear at the top for the aluminum clamp bar.
- **Bottom Bleed:** Leave 4 inches (1200 px at 300 DPI) at the bottom for the roller retraction spring mechanism (do not place text or logos in the bottom 4 inches).
- **Viewing Height:** Keep essential information (QR codes, track names, Wi-Fi keys) between 3.5 ft and 5.5 ft from the ground for optimal eye-level readability.
