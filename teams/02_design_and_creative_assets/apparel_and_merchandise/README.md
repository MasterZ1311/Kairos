## 4. Quality Gates for Apparel Delivery
1. **Pre-Production Swatch:** Vendor must submit 1 sample hoodie and 1 sample tee showing exact embroidery and DTF color rendering by **December 10, 2026**.
2. **Wash Testing:** Sample tee undergoes 3 machine wash cycles to verify zero cracking or peeling of prints.
3. **Bulk Dispatch:** Entire order boxed and delivered to Central Library Storage Room by **January 15, 2027**.
