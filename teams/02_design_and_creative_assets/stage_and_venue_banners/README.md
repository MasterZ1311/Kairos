### Detailed Breakdown:

#### 1. Main Auditorium Stage Backdrop (B1)
- **Physical Size:** **20 ft (width) × 10 ft (height)** | `6096 mm × 3048 mm`.
- **Material:** Heavy-duty Black-Back Star Flex (prevents backstage light bleed-through).
- **Resolution & Profile:** `300 DPI` at 1:1 scale (or `720 DPI` at 1:4 vector scale), CMYK color space.
- **Bleed & Margins:** 2-inch outer perimeter bleed.
- **Finishing & Mounting:** Double-hemmed borders with heavy-duty **brass eyelets (grommets) installed every 2 feet** along all 4 sides for zip-tie truss mounting.
- **Visual Hierarchy:**
  - *Top 15%:* Sathyabama Seal (Left) + AWS Student Builder Groups Logo (Right).
  - *Center 45%:* Giant Event Title: Primary Logo Lockup (`K A [Hourglass] R O S` with celestial hourglass emblem, orbital comet trail in Electric Violet `#A855F7`, and tagline `"IDEAS TODAY — IMPACT TOMORROW"`).
  - *Subtitle:* "India's Flagship 48-Hour Student Cloud & Agentic AI Hackathon".
  - *Dates & Location:* "January 22–24, 2027 · Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai".
  - *Bottom 25%:* Corporate Partner Ribbon: Title Sponsor (Prominent Center), Co-Powered By (Flanking Left & Right), Institutional Grants.

#### 2. Main Stage Digital LED Wall (B2)
- **Display Type:** Indoor P2.5 High-Refresh LED Wall.
- **Resolution:** **3840 × 1920 pixels** (Active Display Canvas).
- **Color Profile:** sRGB 8-bit, 60 Hz refresh rate.
- **Safe Area:** Maintain central 3200 × 1600 px safe zone (unobstructed by podium or standing speakers).
- **Format:** Uncompressed 24-bit PNG, plus looping MP4 motion backdrop (H.264, 60fps, 40 Mbps bitrate).

#### 3. Central Library Foyer Grand Welcome Banner (B3)
- **Physical Size:** **12 ft (width) × 8 ft (height)** | `3658 mm × 2438 mm`.
- **Mounting:** Free-standing MS iron box frame placed at the grand portico entrance.
- **Key Features:** Welcome message, QR code for digital badge verification, event Wi-Fi credentials, and Title Sponsor logo.

## 4. Pre-Press Quality Gate Checklist
- [ ] Document color mode is strictly **CMYK** (US Web Coated SWOP v2).
- [ ] No raster images below 300 DPI effective resolution.
- [ ] All fonts converted to outlines / vector paths (Ctrl+Shift+O in Illustrator).
- [ ] Clear space buffer around AWS and institutional logos verified.
- [ ] Eyelet ring positions (every 2 ft) marked on printer proofs without cutting into critical text.
