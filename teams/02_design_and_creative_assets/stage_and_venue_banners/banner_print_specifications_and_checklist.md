## 3. Pre-Flight File Inspection Record

Before releasing print files to the vendor, Rikish B verifies:
1. **Evergreen Logo Integrity:** Master circular logo on BAN-01 strictly omits specific hourly text in the badge ring.
2. **Color Profile:** CMYK 300 DPI vector scale with 2-inch outer perimeter bleed.
3. **Typography & Outlines:** All fonts converted to outlines (`Ctrl+Shift+O` in Illustrator).
4. **Contrast Check:** Text remains pin-sharp against `#050505` Void Black canvas.
