## 3. Brand Application Guidelines

1. **Dark Background Supremacy:** The Kairos brand assets must **always** be displayed on Deep Void Black (`#000000`) or Deep Nebula Dark (`#0B0616`). Never place the logo or emblem on white or light backgrounds.
2. **Aspect Ratio Preservation:** Never stretch, skew, or alter the geometry of the hourglass emblem or circular seal.
3. **Official Tagline Pairing:** Whenever the primary logo is used in official collateral, pair it with the official tagline: `"IDEAS TODAY — IMPACT TOMORROW"`.
