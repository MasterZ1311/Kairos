With a 5-month preparation window leading up to January 2027, our outreach operates across three high-impact channels under a **Minimum ₹10,00,000 (10 Lakhs) Uncapped Model**:

1. **Enterprise Tech & Cloud Platforms:**
   * Targeting Title Sponsor (**₹3,50,000 – ₹5,00,000+**) and Powered By Sponsor (**₹2,00,000+**) among tech enterprises, cloud providers, AI startups, and recruitment platforms seeking access to 400–500 top engineering builders.
