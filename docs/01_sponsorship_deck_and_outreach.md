## 1. Sponsorship Philosophy & Corporate Engagement Channels

With our preparation window leading up to January 2027, corporate outreach operates across four structured engagement channels:

1. **Enterprise Tech & Cloud Hyperscalers:**
   * Pitching Title Sponsor (**₹2,00,000**) and Co-Powered By Sponsor (**₹1,00,000**) among tech enterprises, cloud providers, and foundation AI startups seeking developer adoption and hiring access to 400–600 top builders.
2. **Track Bounty Partners (₹25,000 per Track):**
   * Technology companies seeking developer adoption for proprietary APIs, SDKs, or developer tools (e.g., Postman, MongoDB, Supabase, Polygon/Solana).
3. **In-Kind Experiential & Product Partners:**
   * **Energy / Beverage:** Product sampling for 400–600 hackers during midnight coding sprints.
   * **DevTools & Tooling Credits:** Free student developer packs, IDE licenses, and sandbox credits.
4. **Referral Policy Notice (Quarantined):**
   > [!WARNING]
   > **`[APPROVAL REQUIRED — DO NOT OFFER]`**  
   > Prior proposals described a crowdsourced cash commission ("Rainmaker Bounty"). **This is NOT an active policy.** Offering cash bounties to students without formal University Finance approval violates institutional regulations and statutory tax laws. Contributor recognition operates strictly through non-monetary academic honors (Presidential Recommendation Letters, VIP Passes, Awards).
