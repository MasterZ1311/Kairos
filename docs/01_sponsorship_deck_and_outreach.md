## 1. Sponsorship Philosophy & The 5-Month Corporate Strategy

With a 5-month preparation window leading up to January 2027, our outreach operates across three high-impact channels:

1. **Enterprise Tech & Cloud Platforms:**
   * Targeting Title Sponsor (**₹1,25,000**) and Powered By Sponsor (**₹70,000**) among tech enterprises, cloud providers, AI startups, and recruitment platforms seeking access to 400 top engineering builders.
2. **Track & Bounty Partners (₹20,000 – ₹25,000 per Track):**
   * Tech companies seeking developer adoption for their APIs, SDKs, or developer tools (e.g., Postman, MongoDB, Supabase, Appwrite, Polygon/Solana).
3. **In-Kind Experiential & Product Partners:**
   * **Energy / Beverage (e.g., Red Bull, Monster, Local Beverage Brands):** Pitching product sponsorship for 400 hackers during midnight hacking sprints.
   * **DevTools & Swag (GitHub Education, JetBrains, Notion, Devfolio):** Free student developer packs and swag items.
