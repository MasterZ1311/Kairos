## 4. Operational Systems & Safety Specifications

### 4.1 Electrical & AV Load Management
* **Primary Source:** Dedicated 3-Phase HT connection to Sathyabama Indoor Stadium Substation.
* **Capacity:** 65 kVA dedicated line for arena bays and stage AV rig.
* **Backup Power:** Dual Automated Diesel Generator with uninterrupted Online UPS backing all 100 hacking bays (<10ms failover).
* **Stage Display:** 3840×1920 P2.5 Indoor Ultra-HD LED Wall with Datapath Multi-Display Controller.

### 4.2 Network Architecture & Security
* **Bandwidth:** 2 × 1 Gbps redundant National Knowledge Network (NKN) leased lines.
* **SSID Partitioning:**
  * `KAIROS-BUILDER`: Bandwidth-shaped at 25 Mbps symmetric per client; peer-to-peer discovery disabled.
  * `KAIROS-ADMIN`: Prioritized 100 Mbps symmetric unshaped for streaming encoders, jury tablets, and webhook servers.
* **Webhook Audit Engine:** Continuous polling of repository push events; automated commit freeze at exactly 08:30 AM on Sunday, January 24, 2027.

### 4.3 Student Welfare & Campus Mobility
* **Campus EV Buggies:** 3 Green Electric Vehicle (EV) buggies running non-stop 24/7 loops transporting participants across campus facilities.
* **Dining Transit:** Coordinated escorted movement to Campus Mess Halls for hot buffet breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
* **Medical & First-Aid:** Dedicated 24-hour first-aid booth in Stadium Foyer with certified student responders, emergency vehicle on campus standby, and tie-up with Sathyabama General Hospital.
