### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The **AWS Student Builder Group (AWS SBG) — SIST Chapter** respectfully submits the official upgraded event requisition proposal for **KAIROS 2027 (Grand Edition)**, our premier national flagship technical hackathon. 

Following strategic alignment with institutional priorities, corporate sponsorship cycles, and national university calendars, the event has been scheduled for **January 22 – 24, 2027 (Friday morning to Sunday afternoon, 48 hours continuous)**. 

To ensure maximum national prestige and open access for top engineering talent across India, **KAIROS 2027 WILL COLLECT ZERO ENTRY FEES FROM PARTICIPANTS (100% FREE FOR ALL STUDENTS)**. 

To guarantee that the engineering quality of participating teams is **uncompromisingly peak**, Kairos 2027 introduces a **Dual-Phase Selection Pipeline**:
1. **Phase 0 (Free National Applications):** Open October 15 – December 15, 2026 (Targeting 2,000+ Teams across IITs, NITs, BITS, IIITs, Anna University, and premier institutions).
2. **Round 1 (Online Technical Qualifier):** **January 02 – 04, 2027** — Top 250 Semi-Finalist Teams undergo a 48-hour virtual code sprint, submit Loom video prototype walkthroughs, and defend their architectures live via Google Meet before an expert jury of AWS architects and faculty.
3. **Round 2 (On-Campus Grand Finale):** **January 22 – 24, 2027** — Only the **Top 100 Elite National Teams (400–500 developers)** advance to the on-campus 48-hour physical sprint at the Central Library Complex.

The entire event is funded through a **balanced ₹3,50,000 corporate sponsorship and institutional capital pool** (anchored by a Title Sponsor at ₹1,25,000, Powered-By at ₹70,000, Track Partners, and ₹50,000 Sathyabama grant), powering a **₹1,75,000 pure liquid cash prize pool**, complimentary 6-meal gourmet dining, custom streetwear hoodies, laser-etched smart badges, and high-density computing infrastructure.
