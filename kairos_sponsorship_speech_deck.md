### STEP 3: THE ART OF THE FIRST MESSAGE — COLD EMAIL FORMULA

Your first email needs to do exactly four things in under 90 words:

2. WHAT WE ARE DOING (specificity — scale, date, numbers)

When they say yes to a call — this is your window. Don't waste it.

**Pre-call preparation — do this the night before:**

- Find out if they recently launched a new product, SDK, or API
- Check their careers page — are they actively hiring engineers?
- Check their LinkedIn for recent company announcements

**Structure of the perfect 20-minute discovery call:**

| Time | What You Do |
